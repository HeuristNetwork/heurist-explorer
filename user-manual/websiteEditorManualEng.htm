Heurist Website Editor

The website editor shows the pages of a website on the left and a preview of the selected page on the right. The toolbar on top has Properties (the website or page record), Styles (colours, fonts and common styles of the whole website), Check (the format of all pages and the records a visitor can not see), Open (the saved website in a new tab), Help and Close.

Site tab: the pages of the website

  • Drag pages to change their order in the main menu of the website; drop a page onto another one to make it a sub page.
  • Add a page with Add page on Home, a sub page with + of a page; × removes a page from the website.
  • Click Header, Home, Footer or a page to edit it on the Page tab.

Page tab: the elements of a page

  • Drag elements to change their order on the page; groups, tabs and accordions take other elements inside.
  • + of the page, of a group, tabs or an accordion adds an element (tabs and accordions: a new panel).
  • The pen of a text (or a double click on it) opens Edit text.
  • The gear of an element (or a double click) opens its panel; the gear below the tree shows or hides the panel of the selected element. The bin removes the element.
  • Select an element in the tree or in the preview. Changes are shown at once and kept until you Save Page or Discard them; Undo and Redo step through them.
  • A click on a link of a menu, of the breadcrumbs or on the logo in the preview opens its page.

The element panel

The panel has two tabs:

  • Configuration: what the element shows (its records, files, caption or module settings), its Presentation (how it appears when it comes into the window: fade in, slide, zoom, or Initially hidden) and its Identification: the system id made by Heurist and a Name of your choice (letters, digits, _ and -, unique on the page). A named element can be the target of the action Show / hide element of an action menu, and its name is shown in the tree, in the lists of modules and in the Check report.
  • Own style: a preset (a style defined once in Styles) or an own style of this element (size, spacing, text, background, border) for each screen size. Menus, action menus and tabs have Item colours here, used when their Colours are Own; Inverted colours suit a menu over a banner.

The buttons on the row of the tabs: ? shows or hides the help texts of the form, the arrow undocks the panel into a popup (or docks it again), × closes it. Docked, the panel is below the tree, or beside the tabs Site and Page when the left pane is wide. The popup is on the left side of the window and closes with a click outside it (the changes stay); Cancel returns to the page as it was when the popup opened, Apply keeps the changes, Dock puts the panel back beside the tree.

Most changes are shown in the preview at once. The configuration of a module (Explorer, Data, Map, Timeline, Graph, Record view, Filter) and its Records and selection are applied with Apply, because the module starts again with them.

Crosstabs (plan 27q2) show a crosstab made in Explorer (tool Crosstabs). In its panel choose the crosstab, where its records come from (its own query, the Filter widgets of the page, the Saved filter links or another module: the latest search counts) and what visitors may change. A Result, Map or Timeline module can take its records from the crosstab: with A click shows the records (set in Explorer) a click on a count shows the records of that cell. Edit in new window opens the crosstab in Explorer; Refresh shows the changes made there.
